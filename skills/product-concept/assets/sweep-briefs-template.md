# Sweep Briefs Template — the designer handoff

`design/sweep/briefs.md` is written for a human designer who was not in the process: each **survivor** expression is a self-contained card they can design from without reading the full record. It is also a work-splitting surface — designers claim cards via the Owner line.

Generate **full cards for Compare/Commit keepers** (and update the selected after Cut 2). Killed expressions live in **Killed at Cut 1** — one line each, not empty cards.

Rules:
- Every full card must be designable *alone*: mechanism context repeated (never "see above"), content named from the noun table, states listed.
- "Bet" and "Don't" lines are mandatory — the bet is what the designer should try to make true; the Don'ts are the principles and non-goals that bite *this* expression specifically.
- On the **selected** card after cut 2 / subtract pass: **Care** is mandatory — what must feel inevitable (empty-state line, the one number, the undo, what stays quiet).
- Status is a living field: `unexplored → killed (cut 1, reason) → candidate → selected`. Keep killed specs in the **Killed at Cut 1** index with one-line reasons — they're quarry.
- **Patch-only once claimed.** The moment any Owner or Status field is human-set, agents never regenerate this file — they patch individual cards (add a kill reason, update a link) and preserve every human-written field verbatim. New expressions append; structural changes are proposed as a diff.
- Header gives a designer everything else in one screen: problem TL;DR, success signals, principles, and links to the record. Nothing in the header requires opening another file to start work.
