# Vision — what design-better is for

This suite puts the **missing middle** back between request and UI: truth, essence, divergence with discipline, and honest gates. It is a product-definition machine for agents and humans working together — not a pixel generator and not a ceremony generator.

## For

- Framing problems from evidence (and pressure-testing assumptions)
- Naming Desire beside pain; sandbox before ideation
- Diverging on **mechanisms**, then composed grayscale expressions
- Subtracting until one path feels inevitable
- Flows that survive interruption; validation of the riskiest assumption
- Critique/QA at the right altitude before craft and ship

## Not for

- Replacing house craft systems (`better-ui`, Linear, Ramp, tokens) — craft is pluggable
- Inventing user quotes or survey theater
- Running every phase on a one-line table tweak
- Polishing typography on a wrong concept
- Feature-matrix competitive "research"

## Bar

Work should feel closer to Rams / Ive / Fadell than to median AI UI:

1. Honesty over decoration  
2. Desire and pain together  
3. Constraints as playground  
4. Explore, then ruthlessly subtract  
5. Care named before craft executes it
